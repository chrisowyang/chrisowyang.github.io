I'm Chris. I build products that use AI to make big systems feel personal —
most recently subscriptions at [Uber](https://www.uber.com/), where I lead
a portfolio with a team of three PMs.

The short history: I grew up professionally at
[Oliver Wyman](https://www.oliverwyman.com/) learning to put structure
around foggy problems. At [Spotify](https://www.spotify.com/) I helped
incubate [Discovery Mode](https://artists.spotify.com/en/discovery-mode),
a sponsored-recommendations marketplace, and worked alongside the teams
building Spotify's internal data science platform and
[Backstage](https://backstage.io/). After business school I went
early-stage: interim Head of Product at Seek AI, an LLM-powered data
analytics startup, where I built the product org and shipped the
[collaborative agent](https://www.seek.ai/blog/introducing-powered-by-seek-an-embedded-ai-data-analyst-for-your-customers),
the [core NL-to-SQL model](https://www.seek.ai/blog/announcing-seeker-1-natural-language-to-sql-model),
and the [Slack integration](https://www.seek.ai/blog/updated-slack-integration-for-seek-ai)
— and stayed through the acquisition by IBM.

Along the way I've advised a handful of seed and Series A teams building
AI products, including [Aleph](https://getaleph.com/),
[Soal](https://getsoal.com/), and [Float](https://withfloat.io/).

I studied at [Williams College](https://www.williams.edu/) (where this
site's purple comes from) and [Stanford GSB](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/).

Outside work, I'm chewing on an idea about
[multiplayer personal software](/writing/multiplayer-personal-software) —
why the tools our households and small groups run on are so much worse
than the tools we each have alone.
